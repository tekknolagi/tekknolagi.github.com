I had an in-person work team meeting last week. In the office, they had
[Bevi](https://bevi.co/) seltzer machines. When Bevi machines first arrived at
Facebook, where I used to work, I looked down on them. They seemed to be a cost
reduction, a sad imitation of The Good Stuff that came in cans and bottles in
the fridge. Good Stuff such as LaCroix[^oncall] and Ito En tea[^tea].

[^oncall]: For a time, I was the "LaCroix oncall". You could page the oncall
    rotation and ask what flavor you should drink. I also deputized myself to
    investigate the occasional LaCroix-related shortage and post internally
    about it.

[^tea]: There was a period where I was drinking several liters of cold jasmine
    tea per day. I stopped when I learned that this could do unexpected things
    to your liver.

But this week was different. I noticed that this Bevi had a *caffeine*
option[^bevi-caffeine]. After some experimentation, I decided that for the
week, I would drink unflavored, chilled, sparkling, caffeinated water instead
of coffee. And it was great. Basically none of the coffee side effects.

[^bevi-caffeine]: Did Bevi have caffeine when I worked at Facebook? Seems like
    no, they only got it in 2022, and I was Not In The Office that year before
    I left for grad school.

For several days straight I thoroughly enjoyed a 100-120mg caffeine sparkling
water.

But all good things must come to an end. You cannot (yet?) buy a Bevi machine
for home. So I am unfortunately now searching for alternatives.

I took a stroll through our local Star Market in search of a replacement. I
looked online, too, since we have a weird selection of beverages at that
market. I ended up with an assortment of brightly colored cans. Time to do a
taste test.

## Requirements

In order to swap out coffee for something else, I have the following criteria:

* no (gratuitous) sugar
* not sweet (no fake sugar either)
* no weirdo other chemicals
* does not taste like jet fuel
* reasonably priced
* easy to dose
* ideally sparkling

We'll judge based on those.

## Taste test

**UPDATE Energy Drink: Peach.** 12 fl oz. Unclear how much caffeine.

Right out of the can, there is a horrific fake sugar taste. It's revolting. I
guess I missed sucralose in the ingredients when I picked it up. Still
disgusting after the second sip and it lingers in the mouth. No thanks.

**ZOA Energy Drink: Lemon Lime.** 12 fl oz. 160 mg caffeine.

Ever so slightly more pleasant coming out of the can but there is still a
chemical sweetness to it. Also missed the sucralose. Gross. No thanks.

**SPARKLING ICE CAFFEINE: Citrus Twist.** 16 fl oz. 70 mg caffeine.

This time I checked before opening. It has sucralose. Ugh. I guess I was
filtering for Stevia at the store. Here goes nothing... ew, absolutely not.

**Liquid Death Sparkling Energy: Murder Mystery.** 12 fl oz. 100 mg caffeine.

Damn. Sucralose again. Ew. Cherry. Gone.

**hi ball energy: grapefruit.** 16 fl oz. 160 mg caffeine.

Okay, this is one of the ones I found on the internet first after searching
instead of one of the random market finds. It thankfully has no recognizable
artificial sweeteners in the ingredient list. I tend to like grapefruit things.
Here goes nothing.

It basically tastes like grapefruit LaCroix, which is a win. No sweetener
detected. Good news. I like this one by and away the best. Unfortunately, it is
expensive, coming in at $3/can.

I intended to do the taste test over a couple of days to avoid
over-caffeination, but that was not necessary because most of them were just
vile.

## Wrapping up and future directions

I also came across **Water Joe** in my search. Seems there was a man in
Wisconsin with similar goals. I want to acquire and sample.

Should I caffeinate my own beverages with liquid caffeine? Seems both less
convenient and more hazardous.

Thankfully this is all just because I like the feeling of being caffeinated and
not because I am dependent. I will accidentally go several days without
drinking something caffeinated and not really notice. I would like to stay that
way, so I am avoiding methods that could spike my caffeine intake and
accidentally make me dependent.

Please let me know if you have any recommendations...
